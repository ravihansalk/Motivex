About Motivex

Honest mechanics. Real results.

Motivex Automotive is a locally owned and operated workshop proudly serving our community. From daily commuters to family SUVs, utes and performance cars, we treat every vehicle as if it were our own.

We give you a clear quote before any work starts, talk you through what we find in plain language, and never carry out extra work without your approval.
